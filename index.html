Jung Han Shin

Postdoctoral Researcher

Department of Brain and Cognitive Sciences
Seoul National University

I investigate how human and artificial systems represent, organize, and use knowledge and experience, with broader implications for brain-inspired AI and cognitive augmentation.

My research spans hippocampal episodic memory, event segmentation, and cognitive training. I integrate behavioral experiments, neuroimaging, and computational approaches to understand how memories are formed and organized.

I aim to connect neuroscience and AI in both directions: translating principles of human memory into brain-inspired AI, while using AI to better understand and augment human cognition.
